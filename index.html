Which camera is this?

Scale is measured per camera, so the app needs to know which one it is looking through. A profile is the camera and its adapter — the same body on a C-mount and on an eyepiece adapter are two profiles, because they give different µm/pixel through the same objective.

Which objective?

Pick the objective on the nosepiece. This sets the scale of the finished slide — you can correct it afterwards if you pick the wrong one.

Set white balance

Move to a blank part of the slide — glass only, no tissue in view — and hold steady. What the camera calls white there is the reference; everything else is measured against it. Stored per camera, so it is once per setup.

Calibrate scale

Put a stage micrometer under the objective and focus on the rulings. Freeze the view, drag a line between two marks you can identify, and say how far apart they are. That measurement is the only thing that sets µm/pixel.
